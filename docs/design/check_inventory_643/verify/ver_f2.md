## Defects found

1. **Latent fail-open on the read side of the certification stamp** (ORT:1824-1843 `_certification_hash_mismatch`). It compares `_compute_sha256(path) != expected` as strings, and `_compute_sha256` answers `"sha256:missing"` for an absent file, so a stamp that recorded `sha256:missing` matches for as long as the file stays absent. Its docstring says "`sha256:missing` is NOT excused here". What keeps such a stamp from being written is the stamp-side refusal OR1-51 proposes to remove, behind the conductor's deliverable check at substep end (WC4-04). Ran (`f2/p_or151b.py`, the refusal deleted in the scratch tree): a `build` step_result passes with the executable absent, and `_stage_meta_certification` reads True. Not reachable at ec2ddda3. Severity if it were: a phase certified with no deliverable.

True for a deliverable the host authors after a workshop leaf (the validator's pure arm, ORT:12838-12850).

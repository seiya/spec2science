@@ review_target_scope
On this target the host-rendered driver distributes each case: it partitions the spatial axes that `tests.md`'s `distribute` line names, hands each call its rank's block, gathers what the calls write, reduces each call's result over the ranks, and runs a case too small to split with every rank holding it whole — so the kernel decides none of that. An argument that carries the input the `distribute` line names for an axis carries the block's count on each call; a count of the whole case, or a global index, reaches an operation only through an argument the Controlled Spec's interface declares for it. A component's published operations must compute exactly what the spec says over the arrays they are handed. On a problem, `__step` works on its block over its own ghost-extended temporaries, and what makes a returned value wrong there is: a stencil read that crosses a partitioned axis with no halo exchange before it, or an exchange without the spec's periodicity for that axis (along dimension 1 then dimension 2 when the stencil reads a corner); a wall side decided by local index, or not restored after the exchange (walls are decided by GLOBAL index — the block's first global index and the case's count — and the periodic and neighbour sides are left to the exchange); a position-dependent value `__init` computes from the local index instead of the block's first global index; and a refusal that precedes a collective and is not first made the same on every rank by a reduction. On either kind, what makes a returned value wrong is a kernel that decides replication or its work from the grid (`__grid_dims`), partitions again, exchanges a component's arrays, decides a wall by `__grid_coord` (a case every rank holds whole has both walls on every rank, which the coordinate does not tell), returns a value computed over its own rank's cells alone where the spec's value is over the whole field (it agrees on one process and is wrong on several, unless it goes through a harness reduction as below), or forms a whole-field sum or mean from an un-normalised reduced sum — a `__reduce_sum` / `__reduce_sum_int` not divided by the `__reduce_sum_int` of the cell count of the cells it summed (a case every rank holds whole, and a block every rank along a dimension no axis takes holds, are summed once per rank holding them, which the kernel cannot tell without deciding from the grid; the reduced sum over the reduced count of the same cells is the field's mean on every grid, a total is that mean times the case's count, an extreme goes through `__reduce_min` / `__reduce_max`, and a reduced count of refusals compared against zero is sound as it stands).
